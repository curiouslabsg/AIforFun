Workshops

Your admin opens and closes workshops. Click one to see its students: My students shows the ones the class list puts under your name, Whole school shows everyone.

Teachers & class lists

People on this list can sign in to the dashboard.

  • Teacher rights: view every workshop and journal (their own students or the whole school), see and reset PINs, download CSVs, delete student journals.
  • Admin rights: everything teachers can do, plus open, close, activate and delete workshops, add teachers and upload class lists.

A teacher's Name should match the Teacher column in the class list (titles like Mr/Ms and capitals don't matter), so My students shows their students straight away.

Use the address exactly as the teacher's Google account shows it. For Gmail, check whether it has dots, e.g. lloydgoh@gmail.com vs lloyd.goh@gmail.com.

Class lists

Students pick their teaching group, then their name from this list. Form class and teacher are only shown to teachers.

CSV or Excel. Columns: Teaching group, Name, Class, and optionally Level (1 or 2) and Teacher. You can pick several files at once. If a file has no teaching group column, its sheet or file name is used.
