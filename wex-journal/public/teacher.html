Teachers & class lists

People on this list can sign in to the dashboard.

  • Teacher rights: read every journal and photo, see and reset PINs, leave a note on a student’s portfolio, download CSVs, delete journals.
  • Admin rights: everything teachers can do, plus create, open, close and delete work experience groups, add teachers and upload class lists.

Adding someone here gives them access. They also need a login: a Google account with that email, or an email + password login created by your admin.
